   The earlier statement that all processing is US-only is superseded by the
   provider clarification. DigitalOcean's database is confirmed in the US;
   the actual Gandi mailbox/processing locations still require confirmation.
   Gandi's current Trust Center lists infrastructure in France and Luxembourg:
   https://www.gandi.net/en/trust-center
   Its hosting documentation also discusses those countries, but the operator
   clarified that Gandi supplies only email here. Do not infer a database host
   or exact mailbox location from generic provider documentation.
   The preview therefore names both providers and separates the confirmed US
   database from potential Gandi email processing in France/Luxembourg. The
   latter is a qualified inference from Gandi's published infrastructure, not
   an account-specific verification. Confirm the actual email locations and
   applicable international-transfer safeguards before publication.
   Gandi's privacy policy distinguishes its own controller activities from
   processing client data. Do not copy tracking disclosures for gandi.net's
   own website into Quick Reads or claim that Gandi's practices are ours.

2. Activity collection, logs and retention
   Confirm whether the planned app sends only account saved-story information,
   or also separate first-party story-opening/completion reports. If the latter
   is active, add the payload, measurement, account association and actual
   retention details before release. Confirm guest delivery remains off.
   Independently confirm whether proxy/web-server/application/provider logs
   retain search queries, filters, IP addresses, timestamps, request URLs,
   user agents or other metadata, and for how long. Saving stories is not a
   complete description of operational logging. Do not invent absent logs.
   Confirm account/favorite/support-message retention while an account exists,
   provider retention and backups, deletion/rotation and restored-data handling.
   Confirm whether any historical guest or purchase/payment records remain.
   Where applicable, confirm legal bases for account delivery, saved-story
   suggestions, security and support; do not invent consent or contract choices.

3. Account deletion and security records
   The operator says all data is deleted. This supports deleting the account
   and associated live account records, but conflicts with an unconditional
   zero-retention promise for the current Apple deletion implementation.
   app/Projects/Quickreads/Api/V2/AccountDeletion.php removes accounts, sessions,
   favorites, activity and associated records, and revokes retained Apple
   credentials. It also creates a limited subject-hash/event-time security
   record to reject Apple proofs issued before deletion. PruneQuickreadsV2.php
   removes these records after 90 days when deployed pruning runs.
   Confirm deployed behavior, scheduler operation and any backup/log exceptions.
   Do not promise immediate deletion of every local file, backup, provider log
   or security record. Do not apply 90 days to accounts/favorites or all data.
   The separate inactive activity/duplicate-request 90-day rules in the backend
   specification also require deployment confirmation before public disclosure.

4. Privacy requests and effective date
   Confirm that the privacy inbox handles access/correction/erasure and any
   applicable consent-withdrawal requests, and the practical verification and
   response process. The preview proposes email intake; no export button,
   analytics switch or guest-deletion screen has been invented.
   If historical guest records exist, confirm how an installation can supply
   its reporting UUID for owner-operated deletion. Do not infer that disabling
   future guest delivery removes historical records.
   Confirm how a parent's concern about a child's information is handled.
   Confirm the effective date and the exact release shipping Apple sign-in,
   email accounts, favorites, search, narration and Settings > Delete account.

WORDING ONLY IF SIGNED-IN READING/COMPLETION REPORTING IS CONFIRMED ENABLED
This paragraph is separate from the current policy; no guest reporting is added.
Quick Reads sends first-party account story-activity reports to our backend to
measure reading, saving and completion. Reports include story identifiers,
reading and saved state, observation times and request identifiers. Signed-in
activity is associated with your account. Opening a prepared story records
reading activity. Finishing is recorded when you explicitly mark completion or
narration completes; these signals do not prove that you read every word. The
reports do not include story text, private reactions, detailed reading positions
or microphone recordings. This reporting does not synchronize your whole
Library or reading/listening positions across devices.
Add confirmed purposes, legal basis, actual retention and applicable choices
before using this paragraph. If guest reporting is ever enabled, the guest
identifier is pseudonymous, not completely anonymous, and guest reports are
not automatically merged into accounts on sign-in; update the policy first.
